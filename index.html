HOW IT WORKS

Everything you need, explained clearly.

BarterBase is a self-custody escrow interface on Base. Your wallet signs every on-chain action. BarterBase never takes custody of your tokens or asks for a recovery phrase.

01

Connect your wallet

Link any Base-compatible wallet — MetaMask, Coinbase, Rainbow, Trust, Rabby, Phantom, or scan via WalletConnect. Your keys, your custody.

02

Create a swap offer

Specify the asset you want to lock in escrow and the asset you want in return. The smart contract holds your tokens until a peer accepts.

03

Peer accepts the swap

Another user reviews your offer and accepts by locking their side of the trade. Both assets are secured in the escrow contract.

04

Trustless settlement

Once both parties confirm, the contract atomically swaps the assets. No middleman, no counterparty risk, no hidden fees.

05

Zero protocol fees

BarterBase charges a 0% app fee. You only pay Base network gas. The wallet confirmation shows all costs before you sign.

06

Cancel anytime

If no one has accepted your offer, you can cancel and reclaim your locked assets at any time. Full control, always.
