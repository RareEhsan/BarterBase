HOW IT WORKS

Everything you need, explained clearly.

BarterBase is a self-custody escrow on Base. Your wallet signs every on-chain action. We never take custody of your tokens.

01

Connect your wallet

Link MetaMask, Coinbase, Rainbow, Trust, Rabby, Phantom, Base Account, or scan via WalletConnect. Your keys, your custody.

02

Create a swap offer

Specify what you want to lock and what you want in return. The smart contract holds your tokens until a peer accepts.

03

Peer accepts the swap

Another user reviews your offer and locks their side of the trade. Both assets are secured in the escrow contract.

04

Trustless settlement

Once both parties confirm, the contract atomically swaps. No middleman, no counterparty risk.

05

Zero protocol fees

0% app fee. You only pay Base network gas. All costs shown before you sign.

06

Cancel anytime

No one accepted? Cancel and reclaim your locked assets. Full control, always.
